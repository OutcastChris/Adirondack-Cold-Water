The welcome email.

Every new account gets this same email, so nothing depends on who signed them. Copy it from here (never retype it), fill in the two brackets, and send. The links inside point the shop at the partner kit, COAs, and training.

Subject: Welcome to Adirondack Cold Water

Hi [contact name],

Welcome aboard. We’re glad to have [dispensary name] carrying Adirondack Cold Water. We make small-batch solventless live rosin in Granville, New York: fresh-frozen flower, washed in ice water by hand, freeze dried, and pressed. No solvents at any step.

A few things to have on hand from day one:

MENU LISTING: List us as “Adirondack Cold Water” with the cut name, e.g. “Adirondack Cold Water - Marcy.” First Water jars are single-source; Base Camp jars are blends. Tier (Private Reserve, Tier 1, Tier 2) is graded per batch, so take it from the jar in hand, never from a saved menu template.

LAB RESULTS: Every batch ships with its COA, and published certificates live at adirondackcoldwater.com/coa/, searchable by the batch code printed on the jar.

STAFF TRAINING: Our budtender guide is at adirondackcoldwater.com/training/. We’ll walk your team through it in person, and it stands alone as a counter reference afterward.

BRAND ASSETS: Logos, cut cards, and menu images are at adirondackcoldwater.com/retailers/. Please use them as supplied, with no recropping, recoloring, or overlaid text.

WHERE TO BUY: Once you’re stocked, we’ll add your shop to adirondackcoldwater.com/find/. Send over the menu link you’d like it to point to.

PAYMENT & RETURNS: Our payment terms are at adirondackcoldwater.com/retailers/files/ACW-Wholesale-Payment-Terms-v1.0.pdf and our returns and product claims policy is at adirondackcoldwater.com/retailers/files/ACW-Wholesale-Returns-Product-Claims-v1.2.pdf. Two things worth flagging for your AP team now: the COD discount requires full payment to land within 2 business days of delivery, or the invoice is replaced with a Net 30 invoice at list price. Also, payment has to come straight from your bank. We can’t accept anything routed through a third party, including QuickBooks Payments, Bill Pay, or invoice payment links. ACH, wire, business check, or cash at delivery.

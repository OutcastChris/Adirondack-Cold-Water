Start Here

The welcome email.

Every new account gets this same email, so nothing depends on who signed you. If you received it, this page is the link inside it. If you’re on our team, copy it from here — never retype it — and fill in the two brackets before sending.

Subject: Welcome to Adirondack Cold Water

Hi [contact name],

Welcome aboard — we’re glad to have [dispensary name] carrying Adirondack Cold Water. We make small-batch solventless live rosin in Granville, New York: fresh-frozen flower, washed in ice water by hand, freeze dried, and pressed. No solvents at any step.

A few things to have on hand from day one:

MENU LISTING — List us as “Adirondack Cold Water” with the cut name, e.g. “Adirondack Cold Water — Marcy.” First Water jars are single-source; Base Camp jars are blends. Tier (Private Reserve, Tier 1, Tier 2) is graded per batch, so take it from the jar in hand, never from a saved menu template.

LAB RESULTS — Every batch ships with its COA, and published certificates live at adirondackcoldwater.com/coa/ — searchable by the batch code printed on the jar.

STAFF TRAINING — Our budtender guide is at adirondackcoldwater.com/training/. We’ll walk your team through it in person, and it stands alone as a counter reference afterward.

BRAND ASSETS — Logos, cut cards, and menu images are at adirondackcoldwater.com/retailers/. Please use them as supplied — no recropping, recoloring, or overlaid text.

WHERE TO BUY — Once you’re stocked, we’ll add your shop to adirondackcoldwater.com/find/. Send over the menu link you’d like it to point to.

Your contact for orders, restocks, and training visits is Jessica Ben-Yishay, Territory Sales Manager — sales@outcastacresfarm.com. Anything else reaches us at hello@adirondackcoldwater.com.

Strong. Clean. Solventless.

Usage rules

These files are final artwork. Please don’t recolor, restyle, filter, stretch, rotate, or overlay text on any of them. Prefer the SVG logo anywhere it scales. And never pair pricing, potency numbers, or health claims with our marks — menus excepted for the data your license requires.

Collection headers

First Water is always navy, Base Camp always green — the color is how shoppers tell single-source from blend, so never swap them.

Cut cards

One per cut, 1080 × 1350, ready for menus and feeds. Each card already carries the cut name, collection, and sensory notes — no text on top. Cards carry no tier, because tier is graded per batch.

Product cards

Tier warning — read first

Unlike the cut cards, these three carry a tier badge tied to a specific offering. Tier is graded per batch, so use a product card only while the batch on your shelf matches it exactly — and pull it when the batch turns over. When in doubt, use the cut card instead.

Social

For your shop’s feed and stories when we’re on the shelf. Tag @adirondackcoldwater and we’ll reshare. No prices or potency numbers in the caption, please.

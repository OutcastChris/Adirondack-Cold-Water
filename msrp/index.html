How to read this

These are suggested retail prices, not set prices. Final retail is always the dispensary’s call.

Prices are by grade, not by cut. Grade is graded per batch, so take it from the jar on your shelf — don’t tie a price to a cut name.

Figures are per 1 g jar. The 0.5 g AIO vape carries no grade and isn’t listed here.

How It’s Made

Ice, Water & Pressure

Four steps from living plant to rosin, and not a single solvent.

Fresh Frozen

Harvested and frozen at peak ripeness. Locks in the living terpenes.

Ice Water Wash

Cold agitation frees the trichome heads. Pure resin.

Dried Hash

Sifted and freeze-dried to protect every compound.

Rosin Press

Heat and pressure release the oil. Live rosin, complete.
